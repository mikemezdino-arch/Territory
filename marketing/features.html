Features

Everything Between A Brief And A Pitch.

Four steps, one sitting. Every piece here exists to force real creative range and then hold it together long enough to show a client.

Territory Generation

Paste a structured brief (audience, product, tone, format) and get three campaign territories back: a concept statement, tonal words, and a rationale for each. They're built to diverge on purpose, so you're choosing between real creative bets. Don't like the fit? Write your own custom direction and Territory analyzes it the same way the generated ones are structured.

Look Profile

Pick a territory and lock its style, cast, and palette into a single reusable object. Every panel you generate afterward injects that same look profile into the image call, so the consistency is structural: a coherent visual direction, held together call after call.

Storyboard Panels

A beat sheet breaks your chosen territory into shots; each beat becomes a generated panel using the locked look. Edit any beat's action or timing and regenerate just that panel, no need to restart the board over one bad frame.

Timed Animatic & Export

Play the board as a timed slideshow with scratch voiceover and a music bed, adjusting seconds-per-shot until the pacing reads right. Then export an MP4 board-o-matic or a pitch-ready PDF, both rendered in your browser, so nothing you haven't approved leaves your machine.
