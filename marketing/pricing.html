Questions

A Few Things People Ask

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel from the billing portal inside your account. You keep Studio access through the end of the period you already paid for, then drop to Free.

Is there a per-seat charge?

No. Studio is one flat $29/mo regardless of how many people share the login — Territory doesn't have team accounts or collaboration features today.

What happens if I hit a daily limit?

Generation pauses with a clear message until the daily counter resets. Limits exist to keep the product sustainable at this price, not to upsell mid-task.
